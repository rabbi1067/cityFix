Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly are complaints resolved?

Response times vary by category. Urgent safety issues are typically addressed within 24 hours, while routine maintenance may take 3-5 business days.

Can I remain anonymous when reporting?

Yes, you can choose to hide your identity from the public portal, though city officials may need your contact info for verification purposes.

Is there a mobile app available?

Our platform is fully responsive for mobile browsers. Dedicated iOS and Android applications are currently in beta testing.

How do I track my report status?

Once submitted, you'll receive a unique tracking ID. You can enter this on the 'Complaints' page to see real-time progress updates.

Need immediate assistance?

Our 24/7 hotline is available for critical infrastructure failures or safety hazards that require instant attention.
